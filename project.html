01 / Context & need

项目背景与空间需求

Context and the spatial need

静态布景难以随表演者、音乐与场景状态变化。BAMOR 将这个问题拆成三件事：材料能否形变、构型能否形成空间、行为能否被描述和控制。

Static scenery does not readily change with a performer, music or a scene. BAMOR separates that challenge into three questions: can the material deform, can the configuration define space, and can the behaviour be described and controlled?
